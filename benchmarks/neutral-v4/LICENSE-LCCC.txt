The text of neutral-v4 (the "context" and "target" fields) comes from the test split of LCCC-base
(https://github.com/thu-coai/CDial-GPT; Wang et al., "A Large-Scale Chinese Short-Text Conversation Dataset",
NLPCC 2020), released under the MIT License below. The LCCC authors also state that the dataset is provided for
research use only ("本项目所提供的 LCCC 数据集和预训练对话模型仅限科研用途"); neutral-v4 is therefore for research and
evaluation only.
